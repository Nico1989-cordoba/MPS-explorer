# What to report: a checklist for a thesis or a paper

> **Resumen (español).** Esta página es la lista de lo que hay que declarar al publicar un análisis hecho con MPS
> Explorer: algoritmo y versión; ε y min_samples con su regla, la NeNA y c_label; el enlace temporal; el test global
> con envolventes; el barrido; la precisión xy y z; la densidad; la fracción no asignada; d50; los clústeres
> marcados; los controles; la unidad experimental y el modelo; y el sesgo esperado de cada lectura. Para cada ítem
> dice qué reportar, de qué columna de la tabla por axón (v14) o de qué archivo sale, y si el programa ya lo da o lo
> tiene que escribir el autor. Los sesgos citados salen de simulaciones propias **sintéticas** que están en este
> repositorio (nunca de axones medidos). La tabla v14 agrega tres columnas al final: cómo se estimó la NeNA
> (`nena_method`), el radio de enlace en nm (`link_radius_nm`) y la ocupancia reexpresada como **fracción del
> contorno a ≤ 25 nm de un centro** (`occupancy_arc_fraction_within_25nm`, P-R103), sin tocar el cálculo de la
> ocupancia de siempre. Al final, la lista equivalente para el benchmark de métodos.

This page lists what a thesis chapter or a paper should state about an analysis made with MPS Explorer, and where
each item comes from. It is plan A8 of the clustering work (the checklist of the clustering research, its section
9.6, and the benchmark's checklist of Phase D). For every item it says (a) **what to report**, (b) **where it is**:
the column(s) of the per-axon table (axon tables v14) or the file the program writes, and (c) **who supplies it**:
the program, or the author. Every number below is labelled with its origin:

- **[lit: …]**: a published value or method, with its reference;
- **[derivation]**: shown here;
- **[own sim: …]**: the project's own simulations, synthetic data only (never a measured axon), with the page and the
  committed run it comes from;
- **[ad hoc]**: a convention of this program.

Every column named here is described, with its unit, in the dictionary written beside each export
(`<table>_columns.csv`, `tools/column_dictionary.py`).

### The global test: what the literature asks and what the program does

The recommended first step of a cluster analysis is to test for clustering with Ripley's K and simulation envelopes,
after accounting for blinking [lit: Nieves et al. 2023]; a null for SMLM must contain the measured blinking, since
random localizations never form clusters and "more clustered than random" would always come out [lit: Platzer et
al. 2020]; on a bounded or curved shape, complete spatial randomness must be defined on that shape [lit: Ward et al.
2021]. The per-axon null of MPS Explorer follows the three: molecules at random on the axon's own membrane, each
with its labels and blinking, through the same clustering, curation and contour, with Monte Carlo p-values [lit:
Besag & Diggle 1977; Schneider & Schütz 2022] and a **global** envelope that decides (the pointwise band is only
drawn). Report the global p, not a crossing of the pointwise band: on 60 synthetic axons with no structure, the
pointwise band was left somewhere in 31 of 60 (H₁) while the global test rejected 7 of 60 [own sim:
`docs/null_and_scale.md` sec. 6.2, `docs/sims/pipeline_null/calibration_null.json`].

## 2. The expected bias of each readout (item 14)

Biases are paired against the **oracle** (the same localizations grouped by true molecule) on synthetic axons; the
regime is the mean spacing between labelled molecules, a property of the sample that the program does not estimate.
Report the row of the regime closest to the data, say that it is a simulation, and give both regimes when the
spacing is not known independently (a calibrated count, or DNA-PAINT).

DBSCAN 25/10 in the official benchmark run [own sim: `docs/sims/clustering_benchmark/benchmark_summary.md`, seed 0, 10
paired replicates per scenario, mean ± s.e.; scenarios in `docs/clustering_benchmark.md` sec. 2]:

| scenario | 1 cluster = 1 molecule | merged | missed | false | K/µm | median 1NN | median r_eff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sparse (~200 nm), ~1 label per molecule (`sparse_lab1`) | 83 ± 2 % | 12 ± 1 % | 19 ± 1 % | 8 ± 1 % | −24 ± 1 % | +35 ± 3 % | +8 ± 2 % |
| sparse, ~2 labels (`sparse_lab2`) | 79 ± 2 % | 17 ± 2 % | 4 ± 1 % | 7 ± 1 % | −16 ± 2 % | +30 ± 4 % | 0 ± 1 % |
| medium (~100 nm), ~1 label (`medium_lab1`) | 68 ± 2 % | 37 ± 2 % | 15 ± 1 % | 6 ± 1 % | −41 ± 1 % | +95 ± 8 % | +17 ± 1 % |
| medium, ~2 labels (`medium_lab2`) | 50 ± 2 % | 49 ± 2 % | 3 ± 0 % | 5 ± 1 % | −49 ± 1 % | +133 ± 7 % | +20 ± 3 % |

Occupancy barely moved in the clustering study's medium regime, −2 % against the oracle [own sim: clustering study,
quoted in `docs/clustering_methods.md` sec. 5]; the benchmark run above does not tabulate it.

## 3. The occupancy as a fraction of arc (P-R103)

**What it is.** `occupancy_arc_fraction_within_25nm` is the fraction (0-1) of the contour that lies within 25 nm
(Euclidean distance in the plane) of the centre of a kept cluster.

- **The arc**: the closed polygon through the kept clusters' centres of mass in tour order (2-opt from every start, or
  the order of a hand-drawn guide: `contour_order_source`). It is the curve whose length is `perimeter_um` and on which
  `occupancy_percent` is sampled, so the two occupancies are fractions of the same length. The 600 nm P-spline membrane
  of the regularity measures (`docs/regularity.md`) was not used: it is not exported as a length, and the reading
  would no longer be checkable against `perimeter_um`.
- **The centres**: the centres of mass of the kept clusters (after the edge and DBCV curation, and without the cut
  clusters when *Exclude cut clusters* is ticked), which are the polygon's own vertices. The column describes the
  measured analysis; the axoplasm panel's discard has no `_discard` twin of it.
- **The radius**: 25 nm, the eps of the reference pipeline [lit: Gazal et al. 2026]: a stated, fixed radius, not a
  cluster size and not a precision, so the number reads the same for every file and every clustering method.
- **How**: exactly, per edge: the points of an edge within 25 nm of a centre form one interval (a quadratic in the
  arc length [derivation]); the intervals of an edge are merged, so overlapping centres never count an arc twice
  (`tools/mps_occupancy.arc_fraction_within`). Nothing is sampled.

**Why it is added.** The paper's occupancy (about 20 % [lit: Gazal et al. 2026]) is the share of the perimeter inside
the 3σ ellipses of constrained Gaussians fitted to each cluster's localizations, and it depends on the number of
localizations per cluster through the cap of the ellipses (`tools/mps_occupancy.py`, module docstring). The arc
fraction depends only on the centres and the contour, so a reader can check it by hand: with K centres on the contour,
no two closer than 50 nm and no fold of the contour within 25 nm of a centre, it is exactly 2·25·K/P = 50 K/P
[derivation; tested on regular polygons, `test_clustering_integration.py`]. At the density of the reference study,
about 5 clusters per µm [lit: Gazal et al. 2026, as in `docs/sims/clustering_benchmark/README.md`], that is 0.25
[derivation]; closer neighbours lower it, since their 50 nm overlap. `occupancy_percent` is computed exactly as before:
the new column is one more reading, not a replacement.

**How to report it.** "The perimeter occupancy was X % (constrained Gaussians, Gazal et al. 2026); a fraction Y of the
contour lay within 25 nm of a cluster centre." Both are per axon; compare groups with the animal as the unit (item 13).

## 4. The benchmark of clustering methods: what to report

When a choice of method rests on the project's benchmark (`docs/clustering_benchmark.md`), report:

| What to report | Where it is | Who |
|---|---|---|
| the generator and its version | `tools/cluster_benchmark.py` (`generate(Scenario, rng)`); the commit of the run | both: the commit is the author's |
| every scenario with all its parameters and their origin | `docs/clustering_benchmark.md` sec. 2; `docs/sims/clustering_benchmark/benchmark_config.json` (`scenarios`, `constants`) | program |
| the methods, their library version and their grid | `benchmark_config.json` (`strategies`, `strategy_params`); grids: `docs/dbscan_rule.md` sec. 7 | both: the library versions are not in the config; the author lists them |
| the metrics, with their definitions | `docs/clustering_benchmark.md` sec. 3 (ARI, IoU [lit: Nieves et al. 2023]; one cluster = one molecule, merge, split, missed, false; bias against the oracle) | program |
| replicates and seeds | `benchmark_config.json` (`seed`, `n_replicates`, `replicate_seed`) | program |
| standard error | `benchmark_summary.csv` / `.md` (mean ± s.e. over replicates) | program |
| which methods are reimplementations, not the published software | `docs/clustering_methods.md` sec. 5 (FOCAL-style, SR-Tesseler-style and ClusterViSu-style Voronoi, GraphCommunity as a StormGraph-inspired substitute); `docs/emitters.md` (Emitters, a BaGoL-like reimplementation) | author, citing those pages |
| cells missing for lack of time | `benchmark_long.csv` (one row per scenario × replicate × method; a missing cell is a missing row); `RESULTS_D0.md` | author |
| date, commit and computing time | `benchmark_config.json` (`elapsed_s`, `timing`, `jobs`); `RESULTS_D0.md` | both |
| the verdicts and their criterion | `verdicts.json` (decision D11: Δ = 5 points, time ≤ 10× [ad hoc]) | program |

## 5. What the program does not report

- The version of scikit-learn, hdbscan or Picasso, and the program's own commit (`program_version` is a release name).
- The axial precision, and the 95 % interval of the NeNA (both shown in windows, not in the axon table).
- The fraction of localizations left unassigned and any density: divisions of exported columns (items 8-9).
- Laboratory controls and a measured c_label: until they exist, c_label is the literature's 14 cycles per label
  (Alexa Fluor 647 in MEA), 26 as the sensitivity column [lit: Dempsey et al. 2011, per fluorophore on glass; other measurements span about 4-17 events per label, `docs/dbscan_rule.md` section 2.1], labelled provisional.
